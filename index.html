Cabo San Lucas — climate context
Avg temperature & humidity · Oct 2025 – Oct 2026
Actual recorded data Oct 2025 – Oct 20, 2026 · Source: Visual Crossing

Electricity at Las Ventanas never drops to zero (roughly $260–$1,100/mo in cooler months, rising to $1,300–$2,000/mo in summer 2026) because even Cabo's cooler winter months average 71–74°F — A/C runs continuously rather than seasonally. Summer 2026 ran hotter and more humid than 2025 (84–85°F in Jul–Sep, humidity climbing to 76% by Sep–Oct), compounding cooling demand, pushing bills higher. This explains why electricity cost tracks temperature and humidity more than it tracks days occupied. Gas, by contrast, is entirely occupancy-driven — it goes to zero in unoccupied months regardless of outdoor conditions.
